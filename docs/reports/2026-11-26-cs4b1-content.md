## What changed
47 files under `crates/` and `packages/`, and `Cargo.lock` (one path crate); no file of the legacy game, no CI,
kit or gate file. Seven of the files are data made by scripts (36,429 lines; not counted as written).

**The content crate, `crates/content/` (new; it depends on nothing, and a test reads its manifest to hold that):**
- `json.rs`: bytes to a tree, in the one cut grammar a content file has (line feeds and printable ASCII; objects,
  arrays, strings with two escapes, numbers; no comment, no tab, no duplicate key, no `null`, `true` or
  `false`); caps on bytes, nodes (16,384), depth (32), number length (25) and names (64); an id is also a file's
  name, so none of the names Windows keeps for its devices.
- `number.rs`: a number's text must be the one a browser writes for that double, and the writer writes exactly
  that (ECMAScript's rule; the even text where two are equally close, read back before it is taken).
- `write.rs`: the one canonical form (keys sorted, two spaces, a last line feed); a file that is not in it is
  refused with the first place it differs.
- `read.rs`, `error.rs`: typed fields with their ranges; one refusal per reason, each with file, line, column
  and field, and an echo of the offending text that can show no raw byte and close no quote.
- `bundle.rs`: the bundle (at most 16 files and 8 MiB; paths `maps/<id>.json` and `movement/<id>.json`, sorted,
  the id equal to the file's name), `load` and `load_file`.
- `movement.rs`: the soldier's movement table: 15 fields, each with its unit and range (walking, the jump,
  stamina, pushing, the slow-down while aiming down sights). No value is in it: his Export is read at the data
  step (step 8, in 4b-2).
- `map.rs`: the map kind: bounds, spawns, shapes (a box, or a ramp that rises toward one side), ground regions;
  its own exact rules (the bounds on whole centimetres, every other corner on whole millimetres, at least 1 cm
  thick, everything inside the bounds, at least one spawn, an id once in its list) and **the aim's reach**: a
  map larger than what an aim point can name (327.67 m each way, 655.35 m up) is refused (his DEPART-4 note).
- `time.rs`: seconds to units, 3,000 units a second, to the nearest unit, refused when it cannot be one.

**The simulation, `crates/sim/`:** `time.rs` (the same 3,000, and 100 units a tick; nothing uses them yet: **the
players' file is main's, byte for byte**); `tests/rules_scan.rs` now also reads the content crate's source.
