`docs/reports/` moved). Prompt: `prompts/review-graphics-engineer.txt`. Notes: `review-graphics-engineer-notes.md`.
Scope: WebGPU and the WebGL 2 fallback, the render pipeline and the GPU budget, in ADR 0002 (the client build, the
Worker ↔ renderer seam), ADR 0005 (the asset pipeline as the renderer consumes it, its GPU section and GPU pass-mark
rows), ENGINE_OVERVIEW's per-frame flow, SPIKE_PLAN's look proof test, and ADR 0003's "### Pass marks" and look
checklist (condition 15). I decide no look; where a draft's text constrains the look, I flag it.

## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1. Does every `[DECIDED]` LOOK_V2 item hold at the Low quality tier (finding 4)?**
  - Why it matters: VISION_V2 §4's minimum laptop plays on Low. Today's Low row may switch the shadows off (and AO), while LOOK_V2 §2 keeps "soft shadows" and §6.5 asks for soft AO. Without an answer, the minimum machine's look is never judged.
  - Options:
    - (a) every `[DECIDED]` item holds at Low. Low may lower resolution and samples but never switch an item off. The Low shadow row loses "or off", and the budgets are re-checked in the bench.
    - (b) Low may drop named items, listed in the tier table, and Mustafa judges Low in the look proof test.
    - (c) decide after the look proof test's per-technique costs (PM-0003-9), with the checklist showing Low beside High.
  - Recommendation: (c), because the costs are unknown today (no source gives GPU times, R3-LOOK-F15).
  - Decider: Mustafa.
- **Q2. Who makes the look proof test's authored models, and by which route (finding 5)?**
  - Why it matters: the test runs in wave 1 and needs a skinned soldier, a three-stage building and a multi-part very large unit as glTF with named slots. Whether LOOK_V2 §8's route (Spline, then glTF) carries a skin and slot names is not researched.
  - Options:
    - (a) run the part of the asset-pipeline research that covers export checks and slot naming for skinned and multi-part models (OQ-0005-AS3, AS4) before the look proof test, then make the models by the route it finds;
    - (b) a session builds the test models by a route the test itself names, as throwaway, and the research follows later;
    - (c) start the test without a skinned soldier, which leaves checklist steps 10–13 for later.
  - Recommendation: (a). It is a small slice of research, and it keeps the look test from deciding the pipeline by accident.
  - Decider: Mustafa and the Advisor, with the ASSET-RESEARCH timing decision.
