## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1 (Mustafa and the Advisor; it extends OQ-0001-5): which rewind cap, now that the honest rewind at P2 includes the
  server's input buffer?** With part 5's buffer of 1–3 ticks, a fair shot at P2 needs about 233–383 ms of rewind
  (finding 1's arithmetic): above 200 ms throughout, and above 250 ms for most of that range. Why it matters: VISION_V2 §4 asks for fair play at
  about 100 ms of ping, and every ms of cap is also a ms in which a victim can be hit behind cover. Options: (a) keep
  200 or 250 ms, and P2 shooters lead by the excess; (b) a cap set from the proof test's measured honest rewind at P2
  (PM-0001-49's p99), with the one-interval tolerance unchanged; (c) run commands on arrival (OQ-0001-6 (a), Source's
  model), which drops the buffer term but needs security-auditor's same-tick fix (its finding 8); (d) start the
  interpolation delay at 2 intervals instead of 3, at the cost of more extrapolation under loss. Recommendation: (b),
  decided after the proof test, with (c) measured beside it (PM-0001-56 already runs both command-timing options).
  Decider: Mustafa and the Advisor, after the proof test's numbers and checklist steps 10 and 11.
- **Q2 (the Advisor): may the netcode proof test fix the profiles' direction split (finding 5) as a proposed
  definition?** The research says only that "the injected latency is round-trip time" (r2:1987–1988). Why it matters:
  several marks (PM-0001-31, -37 and the interpolation rows) pass or fail on this split. Options: (a) half the latency
  and half the jitter each way, uniform (proposed, with that reason); (b) the whole profile on each direction
  (stricter: P2 becomes a 200 ms round trip, P4's latency); (c) leave it to the simulator's builder and record it.
  Recommendation: (a). It keeps the round trip as the research wrote it.
