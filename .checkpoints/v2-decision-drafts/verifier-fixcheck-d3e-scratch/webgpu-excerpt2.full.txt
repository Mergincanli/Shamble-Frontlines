11112: 22. Errors & Debugging
11113: During the normal course of operation of WebGPU, errors are raised via dispatch error.
11114: After a device is lost, errors are no longer surfaced, where possible.
11115: After this point, implementations do not need to run validation or error tracking:
11116: The validity of objects on the device becomes unobservable.
11117: popErrorScope() and uncapturederror stop reporting errors.
11118: (No errors are generated by the device loss itself.
11119: Instead, the GPUDevice.lost promise resolves to indicate the device is lost.)
11120: All operations which send a message back to the content timeline will skip their usual steps.
11121: Most will appear to succeed, except for mapAsync(), which produces an error
11122: because it is impossible to provide the correct mapped data after the device has been lost.
11123: This makes it unobservable whether other types of operations (that don’t send messages back)
11124: actually execute or not.
