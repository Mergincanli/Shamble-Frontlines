## The math (inputs from LEGACY_TUNING.md `WEAPONS` and `UNIT_TYPES`, lines 167–230)
- **PM-0005-25, Warrior kills Conscript:** hits = ⌈100 ÷ 8⌉ = 13; time = (13 − 1) × 0.072 = 0.864 s; at 30 Hz with carried
  remainders the 13th shot lands on tick ⌈0.864 × 30⌉ = 26, 0.867 s: within one tick. Right.
- **Peacekeeper kills Warrior:** Bagpipe 8 pellets × 11, magazine 2, interval 0.85 s; 11 × 11 = 121 ≥ 120, 10 × 11 = 110 <
  120; the second shot at 0.85 s. Right (body hits inside 34 m; the Bagpipe has no falloff fields).
- **Shield:** 8 × (1 − 0.7) = 2.4; ⌈150 ÷ 2.4⌉ = 63; 62 × 2.4 = 148.8, leaving 1.2 HP; unshielded ⌈150 ÷ 8⌉ = 19. In f32,
  1 − 0.7f = 0.30000001 and × 8 = 2.4000001: the 1.2 HP margin dwarfs the accumulated rounding (about 62 × 7.6e-6), so
  the golden case is robust.
- **PM-0005-21:** ⌈100 ÷ 9⌉ = 12 (9 × 11 = 99 < 100, 9 × 12 = 108); ⌈100 ÷ 8⌉ = 13 (8 × 12 = 96). Right.
- **AB-7's other leg:** Conscript kills Warrior: ⌈120 ÷ 34⌉ = 4 shots, magazine 1, so 3 reloads × 3.5 s = 10.5 s. Right.
- **PM-0005-19:** block 0.7 → 30 % inside cos 0.30, 100 % behind; Banzai ×1.3 for 10 s. Matches r1:1373 and `T`.
- **The minute-5 floor (#9):** 5 × 60 × 30 = 9,000 ticks at 30 Hz.
- **The aim range (#1):** cursor reach about 28 m (44 m with ADS pan) against Katana 3.6 m, Molotov 0 m, Bagpipe 34 m.

## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **GD-STEAL. How does a steal meet a defender, and two enemy teams?** LOOP_V2 §4 says a steal is holding E for a few
  seconds; it does not say what defenders standing on the uplink do, or who wins when two enemy teams try at once
  (three-team modes only). *Why it matters:* it sets how safe a lead is (pillar 1 against pillar 2) and is built in the
  uplinks change-set (LOOP_V2 §11, step 5). *Options:* (a) hold E only, presence ignored (LOOP_V2's words as written);
  (b) a defender inside pauses the steal; (c) a defender blocks it and its progress decays (legacy's tech-box rule,
  GE8). For two enemy teams: the first to finish wins, or each pauses the other. *Recommendation:* build the presence
  rule as a data switch and decide in the uplinks change-set from sims of (a) and (b), 3 runs each, in the three-team
  mode, reading steals and late steals. *Decider:* Mustafa.
- **GD-REJOIN. Does a player who rejoins after the grace window get their tracks and money back?** *Why it matters:*
  UPGRADE-SCOPE puts tracks on the player, but the drafts key them by slot, so a long disconnect costs the whole match's
  progress and the bot in the old slot keeps it. *Options:* (a) no, the record stays with the slot (the drafts today);
  (b) yes, the room keeps a departed player's record by player id until the match ends and restores it on rejoin, the
  slot's bot starting its own. *Recommendation:* (b), which matches "owned by the player"; its cost is one record per
  departed player, bounded by the room's joins. *Decider:* Mustafa, with OQ-0005-AB10.
- **GD-PICK-VIS. Who sees a player's upgrade picks?** *Why it matters:* teammates plan around each other's power
  (pillar 4); enemies need to read a strong player to answer it (pillar 3). *Options:* (a) the owner only (ADR 0001
  today); (b) the team too; (c) everyone, through the scoreboard or a visible cue. *Recommendation:* (a) as the start,
  decided together with OQ-0005-AB7: if picks may add raw power, (c) becomes the answer pillar 3 needs. *Decider:*
  Mustafa.
- **GD-CARRIER. Is a carried tech cache's position public?** *Why it matters:* LOOP_V2 §4 tells everyone where a cache
  spawns; a carrier hidden like any soldier ends the chase that makes steals possible, while a public carrier who is
  slow and cannot shoot may rarely reach an uplink. *Options:* (a) public while carried; (b) public at spawn and at
  placement only. *Recommendation:* (a), with the carry speed tuned from sims (cashouts and steals per match).
  *Decider:* Mustafa.
- **GD-BOT-LOOT. Do bots take world pickups?** *Why it matters:* bots fill most slots in the start modes; looting bots
  change what the drop rules do to balance. *Options:* (a) no, bots keep their class weapons; (b) yes, by the human
  rules. *Recommendation:* (a) for the slice 2 greybox ("keep it simple and observable", LOOP_V2 §10), revisited with
  OQ-0005-AB10. *Decider:* Mustafa.
