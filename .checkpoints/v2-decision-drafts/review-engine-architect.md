## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1. Own-movement latency at 30 Hz (finding 2).** The page draws the local player one tick behind its newest
  predicted state. That is the research's method for smooth motion (R1-TICK-REC5), and it adds 33 ms at 30 Hz. Why it
  matters: LOOP_V2 §7's "the shot feels instant" and the gun feel. Options: (a) keep the blend and measure the
  absolute delay; (b) draw the local player from the newest predicted tick, moved forward by the frame's fraction,
  while remote entities keep the blend (not researched; it risks small jitter); (c) decide after the netcode proof
  test's numbers and Mustafa's feel check at P0. Recommendation: (c), with (a)'s measurement added now. Decider:
  Mustafa (the feel), with netcode-architect.
- **Q2. When is the production release model designed (finding 5)?** Why it matters: from the first public online
  playtest, every deploy meets running rooms, and today a refused client cannot recover. Options: (a) an open
  question in ADR 0006 now, answered before the first online playtest, beside the security baseline; (b) leave it to
  the slice 3 plan. Recommendation: (a). It costs one open question now and designs nothing. Decider: the Advisor.
