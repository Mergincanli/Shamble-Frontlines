in the six drafts' budget tables and pass-mark rows, SPIKE_PLAN, ENGINE_OVERVIEW and the README. These are documents, so
nothing was measured. Every number below is arithmetic on the drafts' or the research's own figures, shown in full.
No new baselines exist: no perf-auditor memory folder exists, and this review took no measurements.

## Agreements with other step-10 reviewers (not repeated above)
- infra-engineer #8 (review-infra-engineer.md:51). ADR 0001's ceilings are a tick at ≤ 50 % of a vCPU at p99 and a transport runtime at ≤ 1 vCPU per process (0001:538). At those ceilings a room may need about 1.5 vCPU, so the "case to budget" of 4 rooms per VM (0006:262–267) is not a floor. I agree. A transport mark sized for the density (tick mean plus transport ≤ 1 vCPU per room, proposed) would make the case real. 1 vCPU for about 2,880 datagrams per second is far looser than the density needs.
- engine-architect #7 (PM-0001-27's pinning rule against PM-0006-13) and #12 (BS's Worker load): I agree. Finding 7 adds heat, which #12 does not cover.

## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1. DROPPED-FRAMES (finding 4).**
  - *Question:* how do the pass marks read VISION_V2 §6.9's "the renderer handles a few very large units and their effects without dropping frames"?
  - *Why it matters:* the budgets as written let 5 % of frames run over one refresh with the units on screen.
  - *Options:* (a) a presented-frame count with the units and their effects on screen, at RD High (0 or a small proposed share per 600 frames); (b) the p95 rows as written.
  - *Recommendation:* (a).
  - *Decider:* the Advisor, confirmed by Mustafa at the stop (VISION_V2 is theirs).
- **Q2. SS-BANDWIDTH (finding 1).**
  - *Question:* is the 160 kbps mean a mark for R2's estimate scene or for the stress scene?
  - *Why it matters:* in the stress scene it is predicted at 157–182 kbps, and a fail there sends the tick rule back to the drafts.
  - *Options:* (a) gate it in ES, and in SS gate only the 256 kbps cap, with the mean against R2's formula at the run's counts; (b) gate SS with a new number computed from R2's formula at SS's counts; (c) as written.
  - *Recommendation:* (a).
  - *Decider:* the Advisor, with netcode-architect.
- **Q3. EFFECTS-WITH-PICKS.**
  - *Question:* should the stress scene's 8 active effects per soldier also count the permanent pick effects that a late-match player carries (UPGRADE-SCOPE; 0005 AB-8)?
  - *Why it matters:* the ability step's ≤ 1 ms is set at 384 effects (PM-0004-62), and how fast tracks level is still Mustafa's open question. PM-0005-32 only reports 16 and 32 per soldier.
  - *Options:* (a) keep 8 now, and gate again at slice 4 with the real track length; (b) raise the stress count now.
  - *Recommendation:* (a).
  - *Decider:* the Advisor, after Mustafa answers how fast tracks level.
- **Q4. HEAT-SOAK (finding 7).**
  - *Question:* is the minimum-laptop target judged on a laptop after 15 or more minutes of play, or on a cold one?
  - *Why it matters:* a match is about 20 minutes, and a 30-second run may measure the processor's short boost.
  - *Options:* (a) gate the soaked run and report the cold one; (b) the cold run only.
  - *Recommendation:* (a).
  - *Decider:* the Advisor.
