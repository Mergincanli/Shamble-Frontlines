## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1 (the Advisor): should the CI-token part of the security baseline (item B9) run before the foundation's preview
  step?** Why it matters: the first CI secret arrives with the preview hosting check (SPIKE_PLAN item 6) and the
  foundation. BASELINE-WHEN (b) researches the baseline "before the first online playtest", months later, so B9's
  interim position governs every preview deploy until then (finding 1). Options: (a) keep BASELINE-WHEN (b), and adopt
  finding 1's interim fix as the position; (b) a short B9 read before the foundation's preview step, the rest of the
  baseline unchanged. Recommendation: (a). It needs no new research, and (b) would reopen nothing but would add a
  session.
- **Q2 (Mustafa): does a host's kick bar that player from the room for the rest of the match?** Why it matters: today a
  kicked player who knows the code can get a fresh token and rejoin (0001:361–365). Options: (a) yes: the room keeps a
  list of kicked player ids for its life (a guest who makes a new identity is limited by the per-address guest limit,
  finding 7); (b) no: a kick is only a disconnect, and the host uses the lock. Recommendation: (a).
