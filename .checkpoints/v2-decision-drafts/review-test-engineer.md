## Questions for Mustafa or the Advisor
- **Q1, the 60 Hz margin run's load (TICK-60-SETUP).** *The question:* the margin run in wave 1 uses a sim that has no
  ability and effect step and no economy, objectives or moments yet (finding 1). Should the run stand in those parts by
  burning their time ceilings, or wait until the content and ability spike has added the real effect step? *Why it
  matters:* at 60 Hz the parts' ceilings already take 3.87 of 4.17 ms, so a missing part can decide the tick rate.
  *Options:* (a) stand-ins that burn each missing part's ceiling in the same run (conservative: a pass still holds once
  the real parts meet their ceilings); (b) wait until after the content and ability spike (wave 3); (c) OQ-0001-2's
  fallback, slice 6. *Recommendation:* (a). It keeps the single-run rule, delays nothing, and errs towards 30 Hz.
  *Decider:* the Advisor (OQ-0001-2 is the Advisor's).
- **Q2, rooms per VM and the goal budget.** *The question:* rooms per VM fixes ADR 0006 part 8's goal budget, but it is
  measured on the proof test's stand-in sim (finding 2). Should that budget be provisional until slice 6's run with
  the real sim? *Why it matters:* a light stand-in overstates density, and so understates monthly cost. *Options:*
  (a) a stand-in that burns the sim step share's ceiling in the density runs, with the budget provisional until slice 6;
  (b) take the figure as measured; (c) set no goal budget until slice 6. *Recommendation:* (a). *Decider:* Mustafa
  (money), on the Advisor's advice.
