- READ S-GF2 https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_interpolation/ (Fiedler, 2014-11-30, "Networked Physics" series). Receiver runs no sim, needs no determinism. 900 cubes x 225 bits ~ 25 KB/snapshot: 60 pps 11.6 Mbit/s; 10 pps ~2 Mbit/s. Interp buffer rule: lose two packets in a row; best delay at 2-5 % loss = 3x packet interval: 10 pps 300 ms (+jitter -> 350 ms used), 30 pps ~150 ms, 60 pps ~85 ms. Linear interp at low rate: jitter/pulsing; Hermite (with linear velocity) removes it; slerp for rotation. Extrapolation (200 ms) "just not very good": no physics knowledge, objects through floor. - READ S-GF3 https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_compression/ (Fiedler, 2015-01-04). Delta against acked baseline: receiver acks "most recent snapshot I have received is n"; sender moves baseline only on a newer ack; steady state baseline ~1 RTT old; before any ack, encode relative to the known initial state (flag) or send non-delta first. Unchanged entity = 1 bit. Quantization: smallest-three 29 bits orientation; position 512/m (~2 mm) 50 bits in [-256,255]x[0,32]; 17.38 Mbps -> target 256 kbps; stationary ~15 kbps. (Numbers mostly for the bandwidth unit.) Final moving figure only in video. Sure: confirmed.
- READ S-GF4 https://gafferongames.com/post/state_synchronization/ (Fiedler, 2015-01-05).
