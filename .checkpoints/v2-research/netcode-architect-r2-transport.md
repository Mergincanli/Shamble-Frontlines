  - datagrams: maxDatagramSize readonly, may change while connecting/connected; HTTP/2 has no equivalent limit. incomingMaxAge/outgoingMaxAge (ms, null=none); incomingMaxBufferedDatagrams/outgoingMaxBufferedDatagrams (min 1, impl defaults). Incoming beyond limit dropped from head of queue; outgoing writes backpressure; expired outgoing discarded; oversized datagrams resolved and not sent (silently). Datagrams "can arrive out of order".
  - congestionControl: "default" | "throughput" | "low-latency" — a hint; falls back to "default" if unsupported; "low-latency" is a FEATURE AT RISK "due to the lack of implementation in browsers".
  - reliability: "pending" until connected; HTTP/3 → "supports-unreliable"; HTTP/2 → "reliable-only"; static supportsReliableOnly; requireUnreliable = UDP required (session cannot be established over HTTP/2 if HTTP/3 not possible).

  - sendGroup/sendOrder on streams (long long, default 0): higher sendOrder starves lower within a send group.
  - getStats: WebTransportConnectionStats {bytesSent, bytesSentOverhead, bytesAcknowledged (at risk), packetsSent, bytesLost, packetsLost, bytesReceived, packetsReceived, smoothedRtt, rttVariation, minRtt, datagrams, estimatedSendRate, atSendCapacity}; WebTransportDatagramStats {droppedIncoming, expiredIncoming, expiredOutgoing, lostOutgoing}.
