Caveats verbatim: "not designed to be a fully self-describing format (unlike JSON)" (may fail under deserialize_any); limited support for tag / adjacently tagged / untagged / flatten ("best-effort implementation for #[serde(flatten)], it may be unsupported in further cases"; blocked on serde#1183); "RON does not yet make any guarantees about roundtrips through ron::Value"; names must be valid Rust identifiers.
