### 8.2 What follows at U4r (this brief's reading of plan :740-758)
1. **The setup condition cannot fail at U4r.** No code in the room replaces a view time or clips a shot before U4h, so
   both counts are 0 whatever the build does. A check that cannot fail is evidence of nothing (the advisor's own words
   in the NF-D4 ruling, advisor :353). The pre-check's records say "0 by construction at U4r" (7.2), never "the setup
   held".
2. **The RTT argument holds trivially and is untested at U4r.** The RTT reaches the room only through the tolerance's
   accept-or-replace and the cap's clip (plan :750-752; §4.9 :340-345), and the view-time estimate that reads the pump's
   RTT is U4h's too (plan :799-800). With none of them built, nothing in the room reads the RTT, so the legs cannot
   decide differently through it, whatever the L1 leg's RTT is (333 ms before its first sample: U4c, plan :555-557;
   Some(0) on Loopback: link/src/lib.rs:203-205). No defect on the RTT's path can show.
3. **What the pre-check does test at U4r:** that the two legs give equal per-tick server hashes when the same commands
   run at the same ticks (the comparer; carry 21's canonical order, which "-30's comparer relies on": carries :146-147;
   option B's buffer; every room decision but the RTT's being independent of the link); `late_count` (condition 2);
   the wallclock bite, a mismatch on a valid pair, which needs no tolerance and no cap; the late-command bite, a VOID by
   `late_count`, which needs neither. And the RTT rule's premise: each client's first-sample instant and first-command
   tick are logged at U4r, and the "first command before first sample" VOID can already fire (it rests on U4c's
   immediate acks and L-c6, plan :558, :571, and on the replay clients' wait for `JoinAccepted`, :753-756), though a
   breach changes no hash at U4r. No bite in the plan shows that VOID can fire: U4R-Q10.
4. **So at U4r the pre-check is evidence for the comparer and the command path, not for the RTT argument nor for the
   replacement-and-clip half of the setup condition.** U4r's report and the advisor's reading of
   results/u4r-precheck-30/ say so.

### 8.3 The proposal: re-run the pre-check pair after U4h merges (the advisor's lean, advisor :346-347)
- **Why:** U4h builds exactly what the argument is about: the estimate with the pump's RTT, the tolerance, the
  switchable cap and the per-shot log of their parts (plan :799-800). Only then can a replacement or a clip happen, so
  the setup condition can fail, and only then can the L1 leg's RTT before its first sample reach the room, so the
  argument means something: in a correct build each L1 client's first sample comes before its first command (the
  ordering log shows it), its RTT is about 0 at P0, both legs accept the same view times, and the pair is PASS with 0
  replacements; a client whose first command ran first would carry 333 ms of RTT into its estimate and be caught by the
  RTT rule (and, at P0, very likely by its replacements).
- **What it is:** the same six runs as at U4r (7.2: the clean pair, the wallclock pair and the late-command pair), on
  the merged U4h build, by the same script, its results in results/u4h-precheck-30/<commit>/ (a folder name this brief
  proposes: U4R-Q12), read by the advisor in U4h's review as condition 2 asks for U4r's; -30 stays NOT-RUN until U6.
- **The recording:** first replay U4r's SS P0 seed 4 recording on U4h's room and count the replacements. U4r's clients
  have no `Interp` (U4g, plan :789) and no `ClockSync` (U4f, :774), so the view times they stamp may not be what U4h's
  estimate expects, and §4.15 :481-483's reason for a P0 recording ("a recording made at P0 keeps them inside the
  tolerance") was written for clients that interpolate. If any view time is replaced on either leg, re-record SS P0
  seed 4 with `scenario --record` on the U4h build first (one more run, its SHA-256 beside the old one's), then the six
  runs. Needs a run (day), after U4h merges: N7 in section 11.
- **Its cost:** six runs of the recording's length (about 6 × 300 s = 1,800 s simulated), plus 300 s if re-recorded:
  at 7.3's R values, from about 6 min (R = 300) to about 1.2 h (R = 30).
- **Why not the alternatives:** (b) no re-run leaves the setup condition and the RTT argument untested until U6's -30 C
  part on the real transports (plan :850-851), where a VOID or a mismatch would mix the transports' effects with the
  room's, which is what the pre-check exists to separate first (conditions 2 and 3); (c) moving U4r's pre-check to
  after U4h drops what it already tests at U4r (8.2 item 3), and condition 2 records it as U4r's (advisor :97).
