### B3 ADR 0005 GPU rules 1727-1913 (done)
- 0005:1737-1739 research has no findings on compression, LOD generation, export checks or slot renaming; one glTF mention: three.js example loads a glTF skinned mesh into WebGPU renderer (R3-CHAR-F8, r3 476-480).
- 0005:1748-1755 rule 1 (quote): "**An imported model carries no look; the engine applies it.** A model brings geometry, its skin (joints and weights) where it moves, UVs and textures where it has them, and named material slots. It brings no baked lighting and no ink. At load the engine gives each slot a shared node toon material of that slot's material class (ADR 0003, part 5.a), one material per slot content and never one per mesh. A slot's content is its class (ramp, colour gain, ink weight), its faction for faction colour slots, and its own base and emissive colour, textures, side and alpha mode, ... so an asset keeps its own colours and textures inside a class, and how colour is authored is OQ-0005-AS4's, not a rule." Why 1755-1759: custom GLSL and onBeforeCompile "not supported in WebGPURenderer" (R3-WGPU-F6).
- 0005:1761-1770 rule 2: "**Material slots name a material class.** Every slot's name maps to one class from a small fixed list (for example environment, structure, unit, faction colour, emissive; the structure class gives buildings their heavier ink, LOOK_V2 §2, ADR 0003 parts 5.b and 5.h); the list and the names are this ADR's content data (interface I-8 ...), consumed by ADR 0003. Faction colour slots take the faction's colour at load, as one shared material per slot and faction, or as per-instance colour where a mesh is instanced (`instanceColor` on `InstancedMesh` ... `BatchedMesh` also carries per-instance colour ...).
