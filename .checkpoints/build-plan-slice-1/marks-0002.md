are copied from the draft; words are shortened. "0002:NNNN" is the line in

Abbreviations (from 0002:1205-1259): **ML** minimum laptop (Windows 11, own 60 Hz panel, Chrome/Edge/Firefox; model is
OQ-0003-1); **MAC** macOS + latest Safari (no budget); **MP** Mustafa's PC in Brave (never a budget result); **RD**
ADR 0003's recommended desktop; **RS** reference server; **CI** ubuntu-24.04 runner (counts, booleans, CI wall time
only). **BX** the boundary scene (proof test's throwaway sim + scripted movers: N soldiers in three equal teams,
15 big vehicles of which 4 very large units of 20 parts, 300 live projectiles); **BX-P** BX with the Worker's step
padded to 13.33 ms per tick. **10-minute match** BX at N = 48 local, 600 s x 30 = 18,000 ticks. **P0** 0 ms, no
jitter, 0 % loss; **P2** 100 ms round trip, +-20 ms jitter, 2 % loss. **H** 3 runs of 600 frames after 300 warm-up;
**U** uncapped; **P** presented (vsync on). Sub-1 ms marks run on the isolation-flag build (5 µs timers).

In the real game: BX's stand-ins become the real slices. "Honest N = 48" needs Slice 2 (bots, three teams); the full
stress-scene counts (vehicles, 300 projectiles) need Slice 6 (the stress scene) unless scripted movers are kept as a
test fixture [unsure]; online rows need Slice 3.
