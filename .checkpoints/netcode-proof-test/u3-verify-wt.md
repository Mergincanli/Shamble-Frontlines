## Design problems (how the recommended design would fail or mislead U3)
1. Inbound precision statistic flatters: "due -> waker.wake" leaves out the tokio hop to the EndpointDriver (what quinn stamps) and the reader task's own wake latency before its arrival stamp. Measured due -> poll_recv is already p50 266-298 us in plain mode (scout and my re-run), i.e. over 0.25 ms at the median. Measure on loopback the reader lag with a same-host sender stamping its send time, and report due -> poll_recv as the inbound number; consider the reader on the dedicated thread.
2. The probe's queue shape does not carry over: one VecDeque per direction released at the head (probe.rs:29-30, 83-86, 203) is right only for a constant delay. With per-datagram jitter and per-peer profiles it turns into head-of-line FIFO across all peers. Needs a min-heap on (due, seq), or per-peer queues.
3. The "1 % adjacent swap" differs from L1's as-built R-5 step (held until 1 ns after its successor arrives; l1 lib.rs:14-20), and PM-0001-3 wants the two layers to read one count. At the UDP layer it also swaps QUIC packets (ACK-only, stream and datagram packets mixed), not "snapshot datagrams downstream, inputs upstream" (ADR 0001:581-583). PM-0001-20/-58 then count only if the app-level reorder share is above 0, so that share has to be measured at the app.
4. RNG: "each direction has its own RNG" shared across peers makes fates depend on how the connection tasks' try_send calls interleave. L1 uses one ChaCha8 stream per flow with fixed words per packet (l1 lib.rs:9-12). Use per (peer, direction) streams keyed by a stable slot id, not the ephemeral address.
5. Fate accounting: the clock thread's `UdpSocketState::send` turns every non-WouldBlock error into Ok (quinn-udp unix.rs:195-219), so kernel send failures get logged as delivered. Use `try_send` and log the error kinds.
6. Unplanned public symbol: `ImpairSocket::bind(addr, cfg: L2Config)` adds `L2Config` (plus any profile or control types) to npt-net's public surface, which plan §4.7 fixes. Reuse npt-l1 `Profile`/`ReorderStep` and npt-proto `SessionControl`, or keep the new types pub(crate). Otherwise the symbol diff FAILs.
7. Shared ServerConfig: option 4b (through wtransport's builder) ties the wt-wtq path to wtransport, against "One WebTransport crate per feature, swappable" (crates/net/Cargo.toml). Option 4a with `rustls::ServerConfig::builder()` panics in an all-features build (both providers). Use builder_with_provider in each feature's own code.
8. `with_custom_tls_and_transport` does not set ALPN (only build_default_tls_config does, tls.rs:654) and `build()` panics without TLS13_AES_128_GCM_SHA256 (config.rs:558-560). The design calls it "probe-proven", but the probe used with_identity.
9. "A small datagram_send_buffer_size": below k x (payload + size_of::<Datagram>()) every send is TooLarge (quinn-proto datagrams.rs:35-40). wtransport gets datagram_send_buffer_space (ADR 0001:439-440) only through the quinn feature's `quic_connection()` (connection.rs:411-417). A transport config with datagrams disabled hits wtransport's `unreachable!()` (driver/mod.rs:218-219).
10. Plan §4.4: with ImpairSocket in front of quinn, `Incoming` appears only after the inbound delay, so a Join deadline taken from quinn runs "from the end of a delay". ImpairSocket must keep the first-arrival stamp per address for npt-server, apply size limits before queueing, and bound both queues in bytes, with drops counted (an RS facing the internet during runs).
11. -24's "tick p99 within 5 % of a clean run": if the impairment-off pass-through skips the clock thread or spin tail, the clean run does not pay L2's CPU on RS's 4 vCPUs. Run the clean reference through the same ImpairSocket clock mode and record it.
12. The raw 10 kpps probe as built paces on tokio (6.5 kpps measured). PM-0001-3's probe sender must pace on the dedicated clock to reach 10 kpps per direction, 1,000,000 packets per profile and direction.
13. Timer slack and RT: the advisor's condition should be judged with the recorded switches the scout ruled out by mistake: slack 1 ns via /proc/<tid>/timerslack_ns (safe std, no capability for self), and SCHED_FIFO via `chrt` from outside (workspace unsafe_code = deny rules out prctl/sched_setscheduler in code).
14. max_transmit_segments 1 turns GSO off (one sendmsg per datagram against up to 10 per call on the plain path). L2-vs-plain CPU differs; only P0 through ImpairSocket is like-for-like.
