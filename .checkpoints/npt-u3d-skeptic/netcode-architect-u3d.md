### NA2 (Low): the reorder step carries intake gaps into -3's delays but never into L, so (h) is not exact and the "reverse" has a second (tiny) mechanism
- Where: impair.rs:558-572 (overtaken: h.due_ns = successor's due + 1, :566) with the held datagram's own t_in kept (:430, :591-597); claim at advisor-u3d.md:231-233 (h) and u3d-report.md:96-98 ("never enters L, E or -3's L2 delays").
- What goes wrong: a datagram drawn to be held (1 %: l1 lib.rs:83) whose successor arrives after an intake gap G (a reader stall on up, socket.rs:607; a late pacer or caller on down, socket.rs:465, socket_tests.rs:493-497) becomes due at t_held + G + D_next + 1 ns. Its -3 delay (out - t_in, socket_tests.rs:747) includes G; its L (out - moved due, impair.rs:681) does not. So a reader stall does reach -3's delay for the one held datagram per flow, and an intake gap can push -3's count up with E unchanged (the reverse direction). Size: about 0.01 x min(1, (G - 0.25 ms)/2a) extra datagrams above 30.25 ms per gap with G under D_held (at most 30 ms); moving -3 by one standard deviation (about 20-30 datagrams) would need thousands of 10 ms intake gaps in 100 s. Negligible in practice, but the text says "never".
- Also in (h): "about 28 ms" is this container's rmem_default (212,992 B) over this kernel's per-datagram truesize on loopback; on RS's NIC path the per-datagram charge differs, so the reader-stall length that first drops is RS's own number. The ruling already says "at this buffer" (advisor-u3d.md:261); the report's known-limits line (u3d-report.md:97) does not.
- Fix (wording): (h) "a stall of ImpairSocket's own reader delays t_in, so it enters neither L nor E, and -3's L2 delays only through the reorder step's held datagram (about 1 % of stalls, negligible); below the receive buffer's fill time (about 28 ms here) it leaves no other trace". ### NA5 (Medium): C-U8-6's per-socket read can find no row, and the carried text does not say that is a failed read
- Where: plan.md:1247 (C-U8-6, C-U8-4), advisor-u3d.md:253-262 and :268-270; with C-U6-2 (one ImpairSocket per leg, plan.md:1245) and u3d-plan.md:386 ("'unreadable' recorded counts as present; a missing read does not", §5.2's rule for the steal and Q9 reads, which C-U8-6 is to share "by the same mechanism").
- What goes wrong: /proc/net/udp and /proc/net/udp6 list only live sockets. With one socket per leg, a shell read taken at the leg edges by C-U8-2's mechanism can fall before the bind or after the leg's socket is dropped; the after-read then finds no row for the port. If U8 applies the steal reads' "unreadable counts as present" to it, the run condition "no drop on the probe's P1 socket between its two reads" is vacuous and a P1 drop passes: the pass by selection the ruling forbids (advisor-u3d.md:260-262). - Fix (U8's text): both reads of the socket's own `drops` column while the leg's socket is bound (after bind, before drop; sk_drops starts at 0 at creation, so the after-read alone suffices if it is taken before the drop); a missing row is a failed read and makes the leg INVALID, never "unreadable counts as present"; or the probe reads its own row at the leg's end edge inside C-U6-7's calls (the ruling's fallback). Proof: by reading the texts above; the kernel fact (no row after close) is standard procfs behaviour, not re-read tonight (no web; medium-high confidence). Carried (C-U8-6) as a rule, but this gap is not in it.
