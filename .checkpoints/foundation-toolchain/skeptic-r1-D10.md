- C12 (what fix (a) can break, .gate/skeptic-D10/t5): workspace `doc_markdown = "deny"` + sim's `#![warn(clippy::pedantic)]`: plain `cargo clippy -p sim` only WARNS (exit 0): the attribute lowers a workspace deny of a pedantic lint to warn in local runs; the gate's `-D warnings` still fails (exit 101).
